Part I — The Geography of Power

The Strait of Hormuz is the world's most important oil chokepoint. It lies between Iran to the north and Oman and the UAE to the south. At its narrowest point, the strait is 33 kilometres wide, with two navigable shipping channels each only 3 kilometres wide. There is no alternative route for oil tankers exiting the Persian Gulf. The Suez Canal, the Bab-el-Mandeb, and overland pipelines carry supplementary volumes but cannot substitute for Hormuz at scale.

The economic stakes: 21 million barrels of oil per day, plus LNG exports from Qatar (the world's largest LNG exporter). A sustained Hormuz blockade for 30 days would remove more oil from global markets than all of OPEC's spare capacity. Oil price response: models suggest $200–$350/barrel in a 30-day scenario. VIX response: 60–80 range. The vol sleeve captures this scenario in full.

"China's Iranian crude — estimated 500,000–900,000+ barrels per day — is 100% seaborne via Hormuz. No land pipeline alternative exists. Hormuz is existential for China's discounted crude supply. This is the strategic paradox: China benefits from Iranian oil discounts but US-Iran kinetic conflict simultaneously cuts China's own supply."

Part III — Parchin: The Nuclear Posture

Parchin is Iran's suspected nuclear weaponization complex, located 30km southeast of Tehran. IAEA inspectors have been denied access since 2015. Satellite imagery analysis from 2025–2026 shows continued construction activity in facilities consistent with high-explosives testing chambers used in nuclear weapon design. Iran's official position: the facilities are conventional military sites.

The intelligence assessment: Iran has achieved sufficient nuclear material enrichment (60%+ U-235, with documented 84% trace detection in 2023) and has likely resolved most weaponization engineering challenges. The remaining constraint is political will for the final assembly step — the "screwdriver turn." This is a deliberate strategic choice, not a technical limitation. Iran retains the Opaque Nuclear Deterrent option: the threat without the test.
