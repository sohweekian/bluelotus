Part I — The Carry Trade Anatomy

The yen carry trade is the world's largest leveraged macro position. The mechanics: borrow yen at near-zero Japanese rates, convert to USD (or other high-yield currencies), invest in US Treasuries, equities, or credit. Profit = yield differential minus FX hedging cost. At USD/JPY 159, the yen is historically weak; the carry has been extremely profitable.

The risk: when the BOJ raises rates, two things happen simultaneously. Japanese government bond yields rise (making domestic investment attractive relative to offshore carry). USD/JPY reverses as yen shorts cover. The unwind is self-reinforcing: yen short covering → USD/JPY falls → more yen shorts must cover → accelerating reversal.
